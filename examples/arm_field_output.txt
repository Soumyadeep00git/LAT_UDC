FORWARD-VALIDATE: stress/allowable is ~1.0 where the beam is fully stressed; it drops below 1

MASS: optimized taper 9.8 g  vs  constant-section (root OD everywhere) 12.2 g
   -> 20% lighter, for the SAME strength. The field optimization bought that.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same pattern as the rotor, different domain: optimize the ESSENCE field (section modulus, grounded
in beam bending), decode to EMBODIMENT (a tube taper), forward-validate (fully stressed).

WHAT THIS CAN vs CANNOT DO:
  CAN:    solve the least-mass section field for a determinate tip-loaded cantilever and decode a
          real taper that is fully stressed, with a quantified mass saving vs the naive beam.
  CANNOT: handle buckling, shear, stress concentrations, joints/lugs, or fatigue/dynamic loads
          (none modelled); guarantee global optimality for statically-INDETERMINATE structures
          (fully-stressed = optimal only for this determinate case); or pick the wall/material
          (fixed here - co-optimizing wall+OD+material is a bigger search, not done).
