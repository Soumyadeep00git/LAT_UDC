READING IT
  One solver. Optics, a mechanism, and a market - solved, DOF-counted, and diagnosed with the
  identical code, because all three are just params linked by relations. 'Missing dimension' is a
  structural property of the linkage graph (over-determined but each demand feasible alone), and
  its fix is always the same shape: ADD AN AXIS. What that axis becomes in metal - a gimbal, a
  variable-pitch hub, a subsidy - is the embodiment, and that is handed to the engineers.
