GIVEN: tip load 60 N | arm 30 cm | carbon limit 350 MPa | wall 1.0 mm | min radius 3 mm

For each station x along the arm (root -> tip), the tool reasons:

  x = 0.000 m  (0% out):
    1. bending moment    M = 60 x (0.30-0.000) = 18.00 N-m        [statics: load x lever to tip]
    2. strength needed   Z = M / limit = 18.00/350e6 = 5.14e-08 m^3   [keep stress <= limit]
    3. invert the tube   R = sqrt(Z/(pi t)) =  4.05 mm  ->  OD   8.1 mm  [thin-wall: Z=pi R^2 t]
    4. floor check       4.05 mm >= 3 mm min  ->  keep
    5. verify stress     sigma/limit = 1.00   [fully stressed (ideal)]

  x = 0.075 m  (25% out):
