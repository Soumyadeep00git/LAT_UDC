====================================================================================
ARM FROM PRIMITIVES  -  generic discipline equations, OD & wall free; does it work up?
====================================================================================
given: load 60 N, arm 30 cm | primitives: bending stress, local buckling, mass
       E 70 GPa, yield 350 MPa, buckling sigma_cr = 0.6 E t/D

[A] with ONLY the yield primitive (the frame I hand-posed last time):
    optimum: OD 26.0 mm, wall 0.10 mm  ->  mass 3.9 g
    stress 343 MPa (yield 350)  binding: ['yield', 'buckling']
    ...now CHECK buckling on it: sigma_cr = 162 MPa  ->  IT BUCKLES (unsafe!) - the blind spot

[B] with the yield AND buckling primitives (frame removed - it must respect both):
    optimum: OD 18.5 mm, wall 0.20 mm  ->  mass 5.5 g
    stress 346 MPa | buckling limit 454 MPa  binding: ['yield']

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNPACKED A DIMENSION (confirmed by the run): the yield-only optimum (26.0mm x 0.10mm wall) BUCKLES (sigma 343 > cr 162 MPa).
  Buckling FORBIDS that thin-wall region, so the optimizer works up to a DIFFERENT design (18.5mm x 0.20mm): smaller, thicker-walled,
  now buckling-safe (margin +108 MPa) and yield-limited. Buckling didn't END
  UP binding - it RESHAPED the optimum by ruling out the unsafe corner. Cost: 5.5 g safe vs 3.9 g unsafe.

WHEN DOES IT UNPACK A NEW DIMENSION?
  The moment the objective drives the design into a regime where a primitive it HOLDS becomes
  binding. Here: min-mass pushed the wall thin, thin walls buckle, and the buckling primitive
  was present to catch it - so buckling 'unpacked' from slack to governing, on its own.
  IT CANNOT unpack a dimension whose primitive it does NOT have - that needs new physics added
  (the residual detector only HINTS one exists from data; naming/adding it is still grounding).
  So: it unpacks the LATENT dimensions of the equations it's given; a truly new one is the frontier.
