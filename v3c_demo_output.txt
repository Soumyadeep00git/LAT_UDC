====================================================================================
V3c - GENERAL META-REQUIREMENT ENGINE   (conservation-wall detector)
====================================================================================
For each mission: V1 tunes VALUES, V2 rearranges STRUCTURE; then the engine scans grounded
trade-invariants and, where a conserved budget is over-saturated, prescribes the MISSING
DIMENSION - decoupled from the hardware that would supply it. Nothing is hand-flagged.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROL - a modest mission a value/structure should meet
  V1 (values)   : met=True  exhausted=False  failing=None
  ==> RESOLVED BY: V1 (value)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEEKER - long detection AND a wide search cone  (optics)
  V1 (values)   : met=True  exhausted=False  failing=None
  ==> RESOLVED BY: V3c (missing dimension) - CONFIRMED

  [META-REQUIREMENT - CONFIRMED]  seeker space-bandwidth (etendue)   subsystem: seeker
    grounded in : optics.space_bandwidth_product (in_library=True, descent 0)
    established : solve (optics): even at the pixel ceiling the static field of view < search cone
    budget      : SBP = n_pixels (<= 8192) fixes resolution x instantaneous coverage
    demand      : detect 2500 m needs IFOV 0.070 mrad; the 60 deg cone is 0.84 sr, but even 8192 px cover only 0.329 sr at once
    MISSING DOF : ADD A TEMPORAL / SCAN DEGREE OF FREEDOM (angular motion over time)
    quantify    : cover 0.84 sr in 3 looks -> revisit 0.04 s (need <= 1.5 s: OK)
    hardware    : gimbal | rotating mount | electronic beam-steering  (architecture picks; physics only asks for motion)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BATTERY - long endurance AND high-g burst  (electrochemistry)
  V1 (values)   : met=False  exhausted=True  failing=a_max_g
  V2 (structure): feasible=False  (3 rotors, max worst-margin (none feasible))
  ==> RESOLVED BY: V3c (missing dimension) - CONFIRMED

  [META-REQUIREMENT - CONFIRMED]  battery Ragone (energy vs power)   subsystem: energy
    grounded in : electrochemistry_batteries.ragone_tradeoff (in_library=True, descent 6)
    established : solve (V1 exhausted + V2 infeasible; Ragone frontier over-saturated)
    budget      : one chemistry = one point on the Ragone frontier; energy and power trade
    demand      : burst needs 6990 W/kg; the frontier gives only 120 Wh/kg there, but endurance needs 275 Wh/kg from the same pack
    MISSING DOF : ADD A SECOND ENERGY DOMAIN (energy channel || power channel) decoupling energy from power
    quantify    : energy cells (~250 Wh/kg) for cruise + power cells/supercap (~6000 W/kg) for burst; one pack cannot be both
    hardware    : hybrid pack | supercapacitor buffer | dual-chemistry bus  (architecture picks; physics asks to split energy from power)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROTOR - long loiter AND high dash speed  (aerodynamics)
  V1 (values)   : met=True  exhausted=False  failing=None
  ==> RESOLVED BY: V1 (value)

  [META-REQUIREMENT - MODEL GAP]  propeller advance-ratio efficiency   subsystem: propulsion
    grounded in : aerodynamics.propeller_advance_efficiency (in_library=True, descent 6)
    established : UNPRICED by the reduced model: capabilities() hover power = momentum theory (fixed figure-of-merit), independent of pitch - the conflict is invisible to the solve
    budget      : a fixed pitch peaks at one advance ratio J* ~ 0.8*(P/D); one value, one peak
    demand      : hover wants J~0.12 (pitch ~1.6 in); dash at 45 m/s wants J~0.90 (pitch ~12.1 in) at 10988 rpm
    MISSING DOF : ADD A PITCH-ACTUATION DEGREE OF FREEDOM (pitch as a function of flight state)
    quantify    : a single pitch must span 10.5 in between the two efficient points; variable pitch tracks J. (To SCORE this, endurance needs a pitch-aware/BEMT efficiency model.)
    hardware    : variable-pitch hub | swashplate (collective) | tilt  (architecture picks; physics asks for pitch motion)
