SpecimenLab · sizing engine

Interceptor Sizing Architecture

A specimen x passes through a physics map f to produce mission output y = f(x).
Architecture 1 — fix f (the platform), tune x. Output is bounded by the class of x.
Architecture 2 — free f itself under the physical invariants F. Output is bounded only by physics.
§1

Six models

Each model is a transfer function with explicit ports. They are coupled, not a pipeline — §2 shows the loops. Every model carries two readings: the selective form (Arch 1, pick a member) and the invariant form (Arch 2, the conservation bound with the form left free).

3 · Prop

actuator disk
in Ω (rpm), airspeed V, ρ, D, pitch P, blades B
out thrust T, torque Q, efficiency ηp
J = V/(ΩD) · T = C_T(J)·ρΩ²D⁴ · Q = C_Q(J)·ρΩ²D⁵
Selective: C_T,C_Q from a specific prop, J-dependent. Invariant: T ≤ (2ρA)⅓P⅔ — momentum theory; A, count, ducting free.

4 · Motor

DC / BLDC
in V_bus, Q demand, Kv, R_m, I₀, I_max
out Ω, current I, η_m, heat I²R
K_t = 60/(2πKv) · τ = K_t(I−I₀) · V = IR_m + Ω/Kv_SI
Selective: pick Kv,R_m; solve τ=Q equilibrium. Invariant: P_shaft ≤ η·P_elec, τ bounded by thermal I²R — realization (Kv, poles) free.

5 · Battery

source
in cells S, capacity, C-rate, I_draw, chemistry
out V_bus (sag), E_usable, I_burst, mass
V_bus = S·V_cell − I·R_int · E = S·Cap·V·DoD · m = E/ρ_E
Selective: a pack (S, mAh, C). Invariant: energy density ρ_E ≤ chemistry ceiling (your 400 Wh/kg), power = V·I — topology free.

1 · Aero

forces & moments
in V, α, β, rates, geometry, ρ
out drag, side/lift force, 6 aero moments
F = ½ρV²·A·C(α,β) · trim: T·sinθ = drag, T·cosθ = W
Selective: bluff-body C_d of a quad. Invariant: lift/drag polar of any shape — a wing trades C_d for C_L, changing the whole force balance.

2 · Structures

mass & inertia
in T_max, arm L, load factor n_g, material
out m_struct, inertia tensor I, load limit
σ(T·L) ≤ σ_allow → m_arm ∝ T·L·ρ_mat/σ · I = Σ mᵢrᵢ²
Selective: size arms of a fixed frame. Invariant: min mass to carry the load path — geometry (truss, monocoque, rotor count) free.

6 · Dynamics

6 DOF · Newton–Euler
in rotor thrusts, aero F/M, gravity, I
out trajectory: pos, vel, attitude q, rates ω
m(v̇+ω×v)=ΣF · Iω̇+ω×(Iω)=ΣM · q̇=½q⊗ω
Selective: quad control allocation. Invariant: the real agility limit — turn-rate & thrust-vector slew set by I and control moment (τ, the true "jerk"), not a scalar a_max.
§2

The coupling is a fixed point

The models don't run once in order — they close two loops, so a config resolves by iterating to consistency (mass ⇄ thrust ⇄ rpm ⇄ load) before any trajectory is flown.

§4

Mission criteria

A threat θ = (v_T, a_T, R₀, evasion). The defeat condition is analytic — the interceptor's capability vector must dominate the required interception kinematics:

defeat(θ) ⇔ v ≥ v_req(θ) ∧ a ≥ N·a_T ∧ τ ≤ τ_req(evasion) ∧ reach ≥ R₀

Objective is the weighted coverage margin, not a count — the max-margin fix from the earlier critique, so the optimizer always has gradient:

maximize J = Σᵢ wᵢ · margin(θᵢ) s.t. buildable(mass, σ, thermal, cost)

Secondary objectives (time, energy, cost) resolve the Pareto face once coverage margin is satisfied. "Best performance" = the design whose capability envelope dominates the largest weighted share of Θ at least cost.

§5

Analytical solve — per-parameter matching

Each parameter has a closed-form optimum given the others — a matching condition. The full optimum is the fixed point of these conditions (analytic per parameter, iterate the coupling), which is far cheaper and more inspectable than blind search.

§6

Two architectures — the real difference

Both maximize y = f(x). The difference is what is held fixed. Draw it as a ceiling: Arch 1 optimizes up to the class bound; Arch 2 rewrites f to break through it.

Architecture 1

config → result · "which"
y = f(x),  f fixed,  max over x
  • Starts from a drone (a platform class).
  • Each model emits a selection: this prop, this motor, this pack.
  • Tunes continuous x + operating point (SPSA / analytic matching §5).
  • Answers: "the best tune of this platform."
  • Ceiling: the class bound — cannot exceed what the topology allows.

Built today. Reaches ~51% on real catalog parts; motor Kv is the binding wall.

Architecture 2

mission → config · "why"
y = f(x),  f free s.t. F(f)=invariants,  max over (f, x)
  • Starts from the mission → solves inverse kinematics for the required primitives (v, a, τ, reach).
  • Each model emits a principle: not "a prop" but "momentum-conserving thrust; disk area & count free."
  • Searches the space of mechanisms f admissible under the invariants F.
  • Answers: "the platform the mission demands" — possibly a different class.
  • Ceiling: the physics bound — y no longer bounded by the class of x.

The frontier. The hard part is parametrizing the mechanism space — see below.

The open research question, stated plainly: how much may f itself change, and how is that space searched? The invariants F (conservation of momentum, energy, angular momentum; material limits) define the admissible set of mechanisms — a far larger space than "quads with different numbers." Arch 2 is the move from optimizing a point in a fixed space to optimizing over the space of physics-consistent maps. That is where this stops being sizing and becomes generative design.
