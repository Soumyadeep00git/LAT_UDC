READING IT
  Neither design wrote a conservation law - the wiring did. The only physics authored is each
  element type's constitutive law, defined ONCE: 'effort_source' served as both a battery and a
  pump; 'linear_resistance' as both a resistor and a pipe. Architecture supplies conservation;
  elements supply constitution; the field solver runs the assembly. No physics tree to bridge,
  no equation retyped. A design is now just: choose element types, wire them.
